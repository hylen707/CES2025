# Application evidence summary / 지원 자료 요약

**Status:** neutral evidence map for student/adviser review. Not an applicant-authored essay or a verified activity description.

## Concise English portfolio synopsis

CES2025 source material on post-quantum cryptography is organized into a 12-slide evidence map. A later audit distinguishes source assertions from supported technical interpretation and vendor marketing. A reproducible synthetic inventory exercise explores migration timing under explicit assumptions while preserving unknown and out-of-scope cases. The repository exposes the reasoning, counterarguments, limitations and reproduction steps.

This synopsis describes available artifacts; it does not establish who produced the original presentation or who understands/performed the 2026 extension. It must not be pasted as a personal achievement until the student verifies their contribution and rewrites it truthfully in their own voice.

## What a reviewer can inspect

| Potential quality | Concrete artifact | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technical precision | Correction memo separates functions/assurance | Audit is AI-assisted; student explanation pending |
| Evidence evaluation | Source/observation/claim ledger, vendor memo | Content traceability does not authenticate personal participation |
| Quantitative reasoning | Reproducible scenario model and sensitivity discussion | Synthetic exercise, not real-world impact or security proof |
| Intellectual honesty | Explicit exclusions and alternative explanations | Student must endorse/correct the reasoning |
| Reproducibility | Original export, hashes, code and tests | No cryptographic implementation benchmark |
