# QuSecure and Quranium: comparable questions, different evidence

## Research proposition

The presentation introduces two organizations in a quantum-security discussion. The correct research unit is an attributed proposition about a named offering and function. A shared technology label does not create a common performance endpoint, comparable architecture or an evidence-backed winner.

The [company cases](entities.json) retain source-reported offerings and keep legal identity, revenue, funding and independently verified customer deployments unestablished. A financial valuation or investment recommendation is outside this study.

| Question | QuSecure case | Quranium case | Research consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What is being described? | The deck and vendor release describe a PQC security offering | The deck and vendor page describe DeQUIP-related technology | Start with attributed descriptions, not equivalent product categories |
| What assurance is established? | The vendor statement is not an independent implementation assessment | Architecture and guarantee wording need claim-level evidence | Keep standards, implementation correctness and operational assurance separate |
| What deployment is evidenced? | The broad government-use statement requires a program, date and scope | Integration and scalability statements require versioned records | Distinguish a pilot, test, procurement and operational deployment |
| What would permit comparison? | A named configuration, use case and measured endpoint | The same function and endpoint under stated conditions | Record non-comparability until a matched question exists |

## Decision consequence

Vendor descriptions can identify research questions. Standards can clarify cryptographic functions. Neither provides the other's missing evidence. Compare the cost and constraints of a specific deployment only after documenting the asset inventory, trust boundaries, relevant function, implementation versions and acceptance criteria.

The synthetic timing model contributes a planning sensitivity exercise. Its outputs are not vendor measurements and must not be used to rank these companies.
